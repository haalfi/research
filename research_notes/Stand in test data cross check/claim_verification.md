# Verification of the external report's remaining tool, maintenance and source claims

Scope: the claims assigned to this track from `external_report_unverified.md`. Lu/Miklau/Gupta 2011, the NHS pilot, Beam/Flink/SimPy, DPSynth, JMLR 2026, overlap theory and time-split evaluation are covered by other tracks and left out here.
All sources were accessed 2026-11-26. Labels: **[documented]** means the primary source was read (registry JSON, repo contents via `git clone`, official docs, paper full text). **[lead]** means search extract only. **[inferred]** is my reasoning. **[unreachable]** means the source could not be retrieved.
Repo dates come from `git clone --filter=blob:none --shallow-since=2025-01-01` and then `git log -1 --format=%cI` or `git for-each-ref` on tags. Lightweight tags give the commit date. Branches other than the default were not fetched.

## Q1. Claim-by-claim verdict table

### Takeaway
None of the checked external-report claims is contradicted. Four are only partially confirmed:
- DataSketches released in November 2026 for Java and C++, but not for Python.
- Atheris had one 2026 release.
- SQLancer lists one 2025 approach, CODDTest, not several.
- The ONS paraphrase is fair but sharpens ONS's framing.

The report's "uncertain" caveats on sdcMicro, Anonymeter, PostgreSQL Anonymizer and ARX can be resolved. The first three are actively maintained. ARX's last release was 3.9.2 in Sep 2025.

The report omits two things. OpenMetadata's ingestion package is under the Collate Community License, not Apache-2.0. DataSketches quantile and frequent-item sketches retain raw values, not only hashes.

The one contradiction is of our own earlier notes: PrivLava code is public (caicre/PrivLava, 2025-12-31). Also, ONS WP16 is dated 2019-01-15, not ~2018.

### Inferences
- **[inferred]** For DataSketches, the external report's export warning is too mild. It says "Sketches are not inherently anonymous" and its table says sketches may hold "encoded source values". The documentation, though, shows that KLL/quantiles sketches hold sampled raw values, Frequent Items holds the raw item values with counts, and reservoir sampling holds raw items. Only Theta/HLL/CPC hold hashes or hash-derived registers. Treat a serialised quantiles, frequent-items or sampling sketch as containing real production values. Theta hashes of low-entropy domains could be reversed by enumerating the domain if the hash seed is known (inference; seed handling was not read).
- **[inferred]** OpenMetadata needs two controls before use inside a protected boundary. First, turn off "Enable Storing of Sample Data" and Auto-classification sample ingestion. Second, have licence review cover the ingestion framework separately from the Apache-2.0 server, because it is under the Collate Community License.
- **[inferred]** Two tools in the external report's suggested stack depend on one company's stewardship that changed recently: GX Core (Fivetran since May 2026) and DVC (lakeFS since Nov 2025). Both still release. This is a governance risk to watch, not a maintenance problem now.
- **[inferred]** The external report is weakest on rough-cadence tools. It understates sdcMicro, Anonymeter, PostgreSQL Anonymizer and Jailer, and overstates Atheris.

### Gaps
- DataSketches release announcement on mail-archive.com (the external report's source) was not read. Dates come from Maven Central and the ASF archive. The ASF archive dates for 5.2.0 directories show a bulk timestamp of 2026-05-01, so they were not used.
- The Collate Community License terms were not read beyond the header, so whether it is OSI-compatible was not assessed.
- Theta sketch hash seed defaults and whether they are serialised were not checked.
- AFL++ `dev` branch activity was not read; only the default branch was cloned, per the branch rules.

## Q2. Notes on claims that needed interpretation

### Takeaway
Three claims rest on wording rather than metadata. Hypothesis (verbatim match), the ONS paper (fair paraphrase that sharpens ONS's framing) and SQLancer ("approaches" plural for 2025 is one approach) need care when quoted in our report.
