## Findings (partial)

- EC readability guideline 2009 Annex s.5: "found by 90% of test participants, of whom 90% can show that they understand it. That means to have 16 out of 20 participants able to find the information and answer each question correctly and act appropriately ... The success criteria will need to be achieved with each question. Results cannot be aggregated." (p.26-27). Full text read.
- Knapp et al. 2011 BMC Med RCT: ethics-approved PIS failed user test; 66% vs 15% clear round.
- NUREG-0899 (1982) s.2.6, 3.3.5: V&V objectives a-f; no quantitative pass criterion. Full text read (scanned).
- NRC IN 92-76 (1992): EOP-3 inspections found "inadequate verification and validation" among weaknesses. Full text read.
- Degani & Wiener NASA CR-177549 (1990): FAR 121.315 vague; POI approves by own philosophy. Full text read.
- PIL-S 2014 (van Dijk, Raynor et al., EC): last-minute regulator changes not user-tested; only new products tested; Beime & Menges 2012 effect 'barely recognisable'. Full text read.
- Faulkner 2003 BRMIC: 5 users found 55-99% of problems; 10 -> min 80%; 20 -> min 95%. Full text read.
- 21 CFR 820.40(a) 'review for adequacy and approve prior to issuance' (no criterion); 211.100(a)/(b). eCFR read.
- FDA FY2024 483 observations: drugs top cite 211.22(d) 'Procedures not in writing, fully followed' 201 of 2483 obs; 820.40(a) obsolete docs 2. Spreadsheet read.
- AGREE (original) reliability: ICC single rater 0.30-0.57; raters needed 2-6 (via WebFetch extraction of PMC2900328; text says two to five).
- Watine 2006 Clin Chem: AGREE quality not related to recommendation validity (abstract only; publisher 403).
- GRADE IRR Mustafa 2013 JCE: 0.31/0.27 intuitive vs 0.66/0.72 GRADE (abstract only).
- De Jong & Lentz 1996 JTWC 26(4):507-519: 15 technical writers vs 30 readers: little overlap (abstract only).
