# Six new leads leave every recommendation standing

## 1. Kernsatz

**No recommendation changes, and none of the six leads measures the central claim.** LLM2Alloy compares two documentation routes and one code route on one implementation bug. No prose-derived oracles are set against spec-derived ones on spec misreadings, so the [V §1] sentence "has no head-to-head measurement" stays true. What did change is minor: Amrollahi et al. and Bisharat et al. move from A-pre to **A**, the verified edition's TLA+ sentence misstates its figures and needs correcting, Amrollahi et al. gain a TA flag, and an optional scope note citing Zhao et al. can be added to G8/R17.

Keys: [V] verified edition, [S] summary, [DD] deep dive, [TD] trial design, [PA] prior-art check. TA means the authors evaluate their own tool or method. FT means full text read. Every old-text quotation below was checked against the report file.

## 5. Answers to brief §1 and §5

**Q1 (head-to-head on the central claim).** **No.** **LLM2Alloy: no.** It compares two documentation routes and one code route on one implementation bug, with no spec that misreads the prose. Dai et al.: no. Their oracles come from code (reference executions), not from prose independent of the spec. No other lead attempts the comparison. [S §5] Q1, "Do record-derived oracles catch misreadings that every spec-only check misses?", stays open.

**Q2 (mutation adequacy for LLM suites).** The recommendation needs **no change**; an optional scope note is in §3. Zhao et al. find a weaker size confounder only for naturally sized, white-box suites. Their mechanism (pool-sampling creates size effects) argues *for* keeping the control when [TD] H3 subsamples to equal counts. Their weak within-model mutation–bug correlation fits [V] S7: "Kills guide gap-finding but do not measure real-fault detection".

**Q3 (grades and citations).** **Two grades change:** Amrollahi et al. A-pre → A (Findings of EMNLP 2026) and Bisharat et al. A-pre → A (ICSOFT 2026, full text now read, figures restated). **One flag changes:** Amrollahi et al. gain TA. New optional corroborating citations: Zhao et al. (A) for G8/R17 and, by analogy, for [TD] §2.0's rationale that tests derived from a faulty artefact encode the fault (their Lang-14 example); Dai et al. (A-pre, TA) for [V §2.1]'s shared-misreading finding; Nee et al. (A-pre, MCQ) beside Kim et al. LLM2Alloy (A) at most as a related-work line.

**§5(a).** LLM2Alloy compares **two documentation routes (plus a code route) on an implementation bug**, not prose-derived vs spec-derived oracles on spec misreadings. *Inference*: its direct README → tests arm is itself prose-derived and missed a one-sentence documented constraint in 3/3 runs. That weakly favours per-clause obligations (R11) over a single whole-document prompt, at n = 1 with an unpublished baseline prompt.

**§5(b).** Zhao et al. **do not contradict** Papadakis et al. for G8's purpose. They show a weaker confounder in a different construction. Equal-count comparison stays valid (*Inference*, argued above).

**§5(c).** **Yes, the positives are code-derived** (reference executions; negatives come from program mutants). That I/O harness is the paper's evaluation, not its method. As a spec-blind scenario-derivability gate it adds nothing: G2 already uses pair admission with Lahiri as analogue. The contrapuntal method never consults scenarios, so it maps to Pipeline B / difference witnesses (*Inference*), not to scenario derivability.

**§5(d).** N5 is **multiple-choice** (four-option SycoBench-600). It adds nothing to Kim et al. (349 and 71 models, A) beyond showing that the correlation persists in a 2026 model set.

## 6. Verification debt

LLM2Alloy has four open items: the IEEE version of record and its track were not read; the baseline prompt is not published; the Flipper bug is unconfirmed (repository file not reachable at the tried raw.githubusercontent.com paths, and the bug is not yet reported to maintainers); and Table 2's "34 tests" matches run 1 only, which the paper does not state. Dai et al. have no venue; the figshare artefact was not inspected, and the RQ3 drop in error coincidence has no CI. For Amrollahi et al., the v3-to-published comparison is an automated word diff plus spot checks, and the Anthology lists no DOI. For Bisharat et al., the SciTePress version of record was not read (arXiv v1 was), and the infrastructure rows of Table 4 do not sum to the stated totals. Zhao et al.'s coefficients are not directly comparable with Papadakis's, because suite sampling, aggregation and unit all differ, and no study measures size effects for black-box, spec-derived LLM suites. Nee et al. show no sign of peer review. No lead was checked for versions beyond those named.
